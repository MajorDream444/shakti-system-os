## 3. Sentence Craft

Observed patterns in her own writing:

**Short declaratives, stacked.**
> *"Power isn't the problem. An unconscious relationship with it is."*

**Question reframed, not answered.**
> *"Not, How do I become more powerful? But, Can I trust my own power?"*

**Sanskrit named, then translated — never left opaque, never dumbed down.**
> *"Ayurveda has a word for that: OJAS. The deep reserve of vitality. The glow that
> isn't skincare. The steadiness that isn't discipline."*

That last pattern is the single most transferable device she uses. It is
simultaneously teaching, positioning and voice.

**Refusal as structure.** She defines by negation before affirming:
> *"Tantra is not bypassing. It is not escaping yourself. It is not denying the body."*

`shakti 175` · `tantra 132` · `feminine 118` · `ritual 102` · `Maa 87` ·

"practice" wherever the meaning is disciplined, chosen, sustained.

rather than always writing "the goddess."

gunas (sattva / rajas / tamas).
