# Shakti Canonical Vocabulary Registry

## Purpose

Protect Sheetal Kandola's doctrine, terminology, lineage sensitivity, and professional accuracy across website copy, AI generation, content production, the Living Vault, Temple Library metadata, Claude Design, Codex, and future team operations.

Founder correction takes precedence over historical marketing copy or AI inference.

---

# Governing Semantic Rule

> Do not combine two true facts into a false modality.

Example:

- Sheetal practices somatic therapy / somatic work.
- Sheetal practices pranayama and yogic breath practices.
- Therefore the system must **not** infer that Sheetal practices or teaches a modality called `somatic breathwork`.
