## 1. The finding that governs everything

Eighteen photographs were reviewed on 2026-08-19. **None of them are in the "soft pink,
aged, temple-like" register** Sheetal named as her reference direction.

Her actual visual world is **saturated, bright, documentary and joyful**. Real rooms,
real mountains, real people, midday sun and temple nights.

### The rule this produces

> **The frame carries the devotional register. The photograph carries the truth.**

Victorian-print treatment — cream borders, gold rule, yantra, mandala corners — belongs
to the **frame, ornament and interface**. The photographs stay **unfiltered**.

Applying an aged oleograph filter to a documentary photograph of a real workshop reads
as costume, not lineage. Do not do it.

This is also how Sheetal's own mockup works: a cream-bordered vintage card *around* an
image, set against a dark ground.

### Two corollaries

1. **The pink must come from the interface, not the photography.** The only pink in her
   entire photo set is a tourist sari. Rajas in the UI — borders, glow, ornament — is
   therefore doing necessary work, not decoration.
2. **Red is already everywhere in her life.** Bindi, maroon dupattas, the red circle,
   the embroidered shawl. The website was the only place missing it. The guna palette
   is not imposed; it is observed.

### Why S-00 leads

It resolves more open problems in one frame than any other image:

- **The lotus is ours.** The Threshold sigil is an eight-petal lotus with a bindu at
  centre. Here it is carved stone, and she is the bindu. Nothing had to be arranged.
- **Water and flora stop being fake.** These are the two elements currently rendered as
  CSS radial gradients and rounded botanical shapes — the last synthetic atmosphere in
  the build. A waterfall and real jungle replace both.
- **Rajas without adding any.** Crimson silk against green. The red is in the frame
  already.
- **The gesture family.** Arms open, head tilted back, eyes closed — the same posture
  as S-02, S-01 and S-03. The set has an internal grammar of raised and opened arms;
  the site should use it deliberately.

### Why S-02 is the founder image

The only frame in the set that is unambiguously *her, as practitioner* rather than
traveller or participant. Devotional posture without performing devotion.

Note: her garment is **black, red and cream** — tamas, rajas, sattva. The palette
doctrine and the photograph agree without anyone having arranged it.

### Why S-03 was the surprise

Arms raised to the temple, night sky near-black, stone, gold finials. It is the site's
own palette occurring naturally, it satisfies the "temple" soft touch as a real
photograph rather than an icon, and it needs no face.

## 3. Treatment rules

1. **No aged, sepia or oleograph filters on any documentary photograph.** The frame
   does that work.
2. **S-05 stays monochrome.** It is palette-neutral and sits against any station of the
   luminosity progression without fighting it.
3. **Do not add red.** It is already present in almost every frame. Adding more tips
   from rajas into flood, against the restraint clause.
4. **Crop for gesture, not for face.** The strongest images here are about raised arms,
   namaste, a turn mid-movement. Crop to keep the gesture whole.
5. **Frames**: cream border, thin gold rule, generous mat. Mandala corner ornament used
   once per page at most.
6. **Placement of S-06** — the red circle images are more revealing than the rest.
   Authentic to embodiment work and consistent with her own vocabulary, but place them
   **deeper in the journey**, not on the front door where a first-time visitor meets
   them without context.
7. **Use the gesture grammar.** S-00, S-01, S-02, S-03, S-04 and S-12 are all raised or
   opened arms. That repetition is an asset — sequence them so the gesture recurs as the
   seeker moves inward, rather than scattering them at random.

### S-12 — caution, not a rejection

The Adiyogi statue is a highly recognisable landmark of another organisation with its
own following and public associations. Using it prominently reads as affiliation.

Hold it in reserve for a personal-journey or gallery context where it is clearly *her
pilgrimage*, not a claimed lineage. Do not put it on the front door. Confirm with
Sheetal before publishing it at all — this is a lineage-adjacent judgement and the
vocabulary registry already requires human review for lineage claims.
