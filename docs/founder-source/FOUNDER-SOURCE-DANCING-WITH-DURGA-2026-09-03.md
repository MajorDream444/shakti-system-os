## Verbatim Founder Message

```text
Hey team, I want to share what I’m thinking about creating for Navratri so we can start developing it and have the first launch materials ready by the next week.

*DANCING WITH DURGA Devotion with a Spine*
A Nine-Night Navratri Sadhana Through the Navadurgas
Or

*DANCING WITH DURGA Devotion to Dharma*

A 9 Night Navratri Sadhana Through the Navadurgas
Or

*Durga. Devotion. Dharma*
a 9 Night Navratri Sadhana

This will be a 9 night, women-only Maa Durga sadhana with five live online gatherings. Every day we will meet a form of the Navadurgas through myth, mantra, devotion, movement, dance and somatic embodiment.

Maa Durga is the complete center of this offering. The feeling is bold, devotional and powerful. Almost like a sacred Fight Club or Warrior Goddess Club for women, preparing us to trust our bodies, stand up, say no, protect what is sacred and stop abandoning ourselves.

Working schedule

All live sessions will be from 7:30 to 9:00 PM IST.

October 11: Shailaputri
Earth: I Am Here

Grounding, safety, trust, fear, belonging, the root and our opening sankalpa.

October 13: Brahmacharini and Chandraghanta
Devotion with a Spine

Tapas, courage, inner authority, voice, boundaries and the sacred no.

October 15: Kushmanda and Skandamata
Yoni: My Body Is Mine

Water, flow, sensuality, creative life force, bodily sovereignty, the fierce inner mother and a trauma-aware exploration of yoni sthana.

October 17: Katyayani and Kalaratri
Fire: My No Is Sacred

Durga’s warrior power, sacred rage, protection, boundaries, the Kali current, sound, movement and warrior dance.

October 19: Mahagauri and Siddhidatri
Unity: My Power Serves Life

Purification, love, sisterhood, integration, wholeness, dharma and the closing blessing.

On the four non-live nights, participants will receive a shorter mantra, audio transmission, reflection or embodiment practice. This allows us to honour all nine forms without meeting live every evening.

Central message

Durga teaches us how to feel fear and stay.

How to trust the body.
How to say no without apologizing.
How to feel anger without letting it consume us.
How to protect what is sacred.
How to stand in our power without abandoning our tenderness.

This is devotion with a spine.

Investment

Global:

- Early Devotion: $111 for the first 9 women
- Standard: $222

India-resident pricing:

- Early Devotion: ₹6,666
- Standard: ₹9,999

The first 9 places will receive the Early Devotion rate, whichever regional price applies.

There will also be:

- One fully gifted scholarship
- 2 supported-price places
- Bonuses and a few surprises throughout the nine nights

The doorway into *Shakti Shala*

Dancing with Durga will also become the doorway into Shakti Shala.

Women will first experience the way I hold devotion, embodiment, nervous-system work and community through this container. At the end, the women who feel genuinely aligned can be invited to continue into Shakti Shala.

Visual world

I want this to feel completely and unmistakably Durga:

- Sindoor red, blood red and deep oxblood
- Black and antique gold accents
- Maa Durga, her eyes, lion, trishul, sword and red hibiscus
- Powerful, earthy, visceral and devotional
- No soft, pastel or generic “divine feminine” aesthetic
- Maa Durga herself must remain at the center

For next week, I would love us to have the main visual direction, key artwork, announcement carousel, story assets and initial launch messaging ready.

The energy is very simple:

Durga. Durga. Durga.
Devotion with a spine.
```
