## Classification System

`APPROVED CANDIDATE`

Recommended for founder/team production selection, subject to normal rights, privacy, crop, context, and placement approval.

`REFERENCE ONLY`

Useful for visual, doctrinal, or production direction. Not automatically publishable.

`NEEDS PROVENANCE`

Sacred/deity artwork requiring source, rights, cultural/contextual, and founder approval before public use.

`DUPLICATE`

Duplicate asset. It should not be counted as a separate creative option.

## Sacred-Image Gate

Sacred/deity imagery does not become production-approved because it exists in the founder-selected folder.

- regenerate Devi, Durga, Kali, Navadurga, Mahavidya, or other deity artwork;
- substitute AI deity imagery;
- redraw sacred geometry;
- reinterpret the founder-approved Shri Yantra;
- infer publication rights from local possession;
- make every page look like Dancing with Durga.
