# Dancing with Durga - Visual Direction

Status: HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED

## Founder Direction

Maa Durga herself must remain at the center.

The visual world should feel:

- completely and unmistakably Durga
- bold
- devotional
- powerful
- earthy
- visceral

## Palette

- sindoor red
- blood red
- deep oxblood
- black
- antique gold

## Symbols

Use sparingly and with a clear job:

- Durga eyes: presence and gaze
- lion: courage and protection
- trishul: sacred force
- sword: discernment and boundary
- red hibiscus: devotional offering
- threshold/door: entry into Shakti Shala

## Do Not Use

- soft pastel divine feminine aesthetic
- generic goddess clip art
- sacred-symbol wallpaper
- generated deity imagery as final art without approval
- fake luxury styling
