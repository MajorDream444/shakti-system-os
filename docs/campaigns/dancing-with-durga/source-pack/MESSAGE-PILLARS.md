# Dancing with Durga - Message Pillars

Status: HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED

## Core Thesis

Devotion with a spine.

## Central Message

Durga teaches us:

- how to feel fear and stay
- how to trust the body
- how to say no without apologizing
- how to feel anger without letting it consume us
- how to protect what is sacred
- how to stand in power without abandoning tenderness

## Offer Promise Language

Use carefully:

> A nine-night, women-only Maa Durga sadhana through the Navadurgas.

> Meet Durga through myth, mantra, devotion, movement, dance, and somatic embodiment.

> A devotional container for women learning to trust the body, stand up, say no, protect what is sacred, and stop abandoning themselves.
