## Annual Anchors

Approved structure can hold:

- Navratri periods
- retreats and in-person immersions
- founder-approved seasonal containers
- public teaching runways
- Shala openings or community rhythms

This does not mean every anchor is confirmed every year.

## Recurring Rhythm

Potential recurring layers:

- New Moon
- Full Moon
- monthly immersion
- weekly or shorter transmissions
- community integration windows

Boundary:

Moon rhythm provides context, not authority. It must not automate spiritual discernment, access approval, or private reflection analysis.

## Memory Layer

The annual rhythm may connect to:

- recordings
- practices
- source teachings
- Vault / Temple Library
- approved campaign assets
- participant-access periods after paid containers

Boundary:

The system remembers approved teaching and continuity. It does not surveil seekers or publish private material.

## Pathway Relationship

```text
public teaching
-> seasonal container
-> Shakti Shala
-> deeper proximity
-> retreat / immersion
-> continuing library and practice memory
```

This is an architectural pathway, not an automatic entitlement ladder.
