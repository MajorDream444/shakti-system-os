# Portal Visual Boundary

Source: Sept. 13 relayed founder visual acceptance; approved 12I/12J lineage remains controlling.

- Use organic environmental contours for meaningful choices, not every section or utility control.
- Contours are non-sacred SVG paths. They are not Shri Yantra or representations of spiritual beings.
- Actual waterfall photography supplies environmental texture; dark red, water blue, warm gold and leaf green distinguish surfaces without changing offer or pathway meaning.
- Text stays outside the SVG mask. Focus outlines stay outside clipping. Whole buttons remain keyboard and touch targets.
- Home pathway titles: 32px; supporting copy: 18px. Begin pace labels: 28px; other response/support text: 18px. Fixed rem sizing, zero letter spacing for meaning-bearing response text.
- The initial one-time contour treatment was rejected. Current replacement uses translucent material, multiple refracted/specular edge layers and continuous 11-19 second CSS material movement. A Pause/Resume control is mandatory. No particles, new pointer tracking, WebGL or added dependency.
- Reduced motion disables all portal animation and response entrance/selection movement. Existing wider application motion is not represented as newly remediated.
- Remove ordinal numbers: these are doorways, not numbered steps. Lucide Flower2, Waves, Flame and Leaf are provisional non-sacred review marks, pending exact founder-approved equivalents. Do not attribute sacred meaning to the library icons.
- Acceptance requires the local side-by-side comparison against `Sheetal Approved Feeling.png`; implementation of layers is not proof of visual equivalence.
- Production Shri Yantra remains held until an exact asset and publication provenance are approved. Do not use the concept image as a substitute.
