```text
provocative truth
→ personal or cultural tension
→ doctrinal / Tantric framing
→ embodied meaning
→ direct question or practice
```

Examples of recurring voice characteristics:

- direct rather than abstract
- embodied rather than intellectual-only
- culturally located rather than generic
- devotional without becoming passive
- willing to use humor, sensuality, anger, contradiction, and modern language

- uses questions as thresholds

The website should not sound more sanitized than Sheetal's actual voice.

Clinical clarity is useful for orientation, but the system must retain her directness, irreverence, heat, cultural specificity, and devotional intensity.

# 6. UX / Design Implications

The August walkthrough exposed that the architecture exists but the experience is under-signposted and visually too continuous in dark-red territory.

Instagram evidence adds an important correction:

Sheetal's world is not only shadow / Kali / dark sanctuary.

Her published work includes:

- vivid red
- pink
- gold
- green
- water
- rivers
- flowers
- body
- Goddess imagery
- mountains
- pilgrimage
- sensuality
- movement
- nature
- temple symbolism

## Updated visual doctrine

> Dark sanctuary grounds the work. Luminous Shakti opens the journey.

> Preserve the depth. Reveal more of the beauty.

Use dark stone, ember, shadow and mountain atmosphere as grounding architecture.

Allow luminous Shakti to emerge through:

- gold
- pink
- red
- lush green
- flowers
- water
- photography
- sacred geometry
- goddess-linked environmental variation
- Himalayan light

The visual system should express integration of dark/light feminine rather than forcing every room into one dark palette.
